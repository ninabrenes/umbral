## 1. clinical protocols and preparation science

### 1.1 johns hopkins center for psychedelic and consciousness research

the gold standard. 20+ years of FDA-approved psilocybin research.

**preparation protocol:**
- 2-3 preparatory meetings totaling ~8 hours of face-to-face contact before dosing
- two-guide model (typically one male, one female) — unconditional positive regard
- rapport and trust-building is the single most important safety variable
- psychological preparation: education about the range of possible experiences
- intention setting (held loosely, not rigidly goal-directed)
- the core instruction: **"trust, let go, be open"** (Bill Richards)
- "if you see a door, open it. if you see a mountain, climb it."
- practical logistics: session room is living-room style, not clinical. eyeshades + curated music. 6-8 hours duration.

**integration protocol:**
- 2-3 integration meetings after each dosing session
- first session: the day after
- narrative processing — guides listen, help make meaning, do NOT interpret
- non-directive: not CBT, not psychoanalytic. supportive presence.
- journaling encouraged in the days following
- meditation/contemplative practice recommended
- follow-up assessments at 1, 3, 6, 12+ months

**screening criteria:**
- psychiatric exclusions: personal history of psychosis, family history of psychosis (first/second degree), bipolar I/II, active suicidal ideation, current substance use disorder, dissociative disorders
- medical exclusions: uncontrolled hypertension, significant cardiovascular disease, seizure disorders, hepatic/renal impairment, pregnancy
- medication exclusions: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, lithium, serotonergic medications (require medical taper with washout periods)

**dosing:**
- high dose (therapeutic): 25-30 mg/70 kg synthetic psilocybin (~3.5-5g dried P. cubensis equivalent)
- moderate: 20 mg/70 kg
- low (active placebo): 1-5 mg/70 kg

**session structure:**
| time | event |
|---|---|
| morning | arrival, check-in, vital signs |
| ~9:00 AM | psilocybin administered orally (capsule) |
| 0-30 min | onset. participant reclines, eyeshades on, music begins |
| 30-60 min | effects begin. guides present, quiet |
| 1-3 hours | ascent to peak. challenging experiences most common here |
| 3-5 hours | peak and plateau. deepest experiences |
| 5-7 hours | gradual descent. gentler music. may remove eyeshades |
| 7-8 hours | resolution. verbalize experience to guides |
| next day | first integration session |

**key researchers:**
- Roland Griffiths, Ph.D. (1946-2023) — founder, led the modern psilocybin research revival
- Matthew Johnson, Ph.D. — safety guidelines, smoking cessation, now at Sheppard Pratt
- Bill Richards, Ph.D. — developed music program and "trust, let go, be open"
- Albert Garcia-Romeu, Ph.D. — smoking cessation and substance use
- Frederick Barrett, Ph.D. — neuroimaging and music research

**key papers:**
- Johnson, M.W., Richards, W.A., & Griffiths, R.R. (2008). ["Human hallucinogen research: guidelines for safety."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18593735/) *Journal of Psychopharmacology*, 22(6), 603-620. — THE foundational safety paper.
- Griffiths, R.R., et al. (2006). ["Psilocybin can occasion mystical-type experiences..."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16826400/) *Psychopharmacology*, 187, 268-283.
- Griffiths, R.R., et al. (2016). ["Psilocybin produces substantial and sustained decreases in depression and anxiety in patients with life-threatening cancer..."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27909165/) *Journal of Psychopharmacology*, 30(12), 1181-1197.

### 1.2 tim ferriss — practical preparation frameworks

ferriss donated ~$2M and helped raise ~$2-3M more to launch the JHU center (2019). his frameworks are the most publicly detailed preparation protocols available.

**4-week preparation framework:**

| timing | actions |
|---|---|
| 4 weeks before | begin daily meditation (20 min), journaling on intentions, schedule integration therapy, medical screening, dietary cleanup |
| 2 weeks before | eliminate alcohol, taper caffeine, increase meditation to 30+ min, refine intentions, confirm logistics |
| day before | light meals, early sleep, no screens after 8pm, light movement, review intentions |
| day of | light/no breakfast (nausea management), arrive early, brief conversation with facilitator, phone off |
| day after | raw journaling (10-20 pages), gentle movement, nature, no major conversations with uninitiated people |
| weeks 1-4 after | weekly integration therapy, continued meditation, review journal notes, identify 2-3 actionable insights, NO major life decisions for 2+ weeks |

**harm reduction — hard contraindications (ferriss):**
- family history of schizophrenia/psychotic disorders (first-degree)
- current lithium use (seizure risk)
- current SSRIs/SNRIs (serotonin syndrome risk, blunted effects)
- under 25 (prefrontal cortex still developing)
- active suicidal ideation without clinical support
- anyone being pressured or doing it to "fit in"

**his rule:** "if you have any doubt, don't."

### 1.3 paul stamets — mycology and the stamets stack

**the stamets stack (microdosing protocol):**
- psilocybin: 0.1-0.3g dried mushroom (sub-perceptual)
- lion's mane: 500-2000mg (NGF stimulation)
- niacin (flush form): 100-200mg (vasodilation, peripheral distribution)
- schedule: 4 days on / 3 days off, cycle 4 weeks on / 2-4 weeks off

**rationale:** psilocybin initiates new neural connections, lion's mane cements them via NGF, niacin distributes to periphery.

**important caveat:** the stack has NOT been validated by peer-reviewed RCTs as a combined protocol. the UBC observational study (Rootman et al., 2022) showed self-reported improvements but was not placebo-controlled.

**species and potency:**
- P. cubensis: most common, moderate potency, recommended for beginners
- P. azurescens: most potent known species (up to 1.8% psilocybin by dry weight), discovered by Stamets
- potency varies WITHIN species as much as between — analytical testing (HPLC) is essential
- "a gram is a gram" is dangerous across species. 1g P. azurescens ≈ 3-5x stronger than 1g P. cubensis

**key papers:**
- Rootman, J.M., Kryskow, P., Harvey, K., Stamets, P., et al. (2021). ["Adults who microdose psychedelics report health related motivations..."](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-01811-4) *Scientific Reports*.
- Stamets, P. (1996). *Psilocybin Mushrooms of the World*. — definitive identification guide

johnson ran what he calls "the most quantified psychedelic experiment in history" in 2025.

**protocol:**
- dose: 5g dried Psilocybe cubensis (B+ strain), containing 24.9mg psilocybin
- frequency: monthly for 3 months
- biomarkers tracked: 249

**results:**
- systemic inflammation dropped 35%+ (from elite to undetectable)
- blood sugar control improved from top 2% to top 0.2% of population
- brain activity data mirrored subjective experience

## 2. neuroscience of psilocybin

### 2.1 mechanism of action (huberman, carhart-harris)

**the pathway:**
1. psilocybin (prodrug) → converted to psilocin in the body
2. psilocin binds primarily to serotonin 2A (5-HT2A) receptors
3. these receptors are concentrated in prefrontal cortex (layer 5 pyramidal neurons)
4. activation increases lateral connectivity between brain areas that don't normally communicate

**default mode network (DMN):**
- the "ego network" — self-referential thinking, autobiographical narrative, rumination
- psilocybin REDUCES DMN activity → rigid thought patterns loosen
- hyperactive DMN is associated with depression (locked into negative self-referential loops)

**the REBUS model** (Carhart-Harris):
- Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics
- top-down predictions (entrenched beliefs) relax their precision weighting
- bottom-up sensory and emotional information floods in
- allows reorganization of cognitive patterns

**neuroplasticity:**
- structural changes: dendritic spine growth, increased BDNF, synaptogenesis
- a single high-dose session produces lasting increases in dendritic complexity in prefrontal cortex
- changes observable for weeks to months in animal models
- the neuroplasticity window extends significantly beyond the acute experience

**the therapeutic window (24-72 hours post-session):**
- enhanced neuroplasticity — brain is more receptive to forming new connections
- what you do during this window has outsized impact
- recommendations: low stimulation, reflective practices, quality sleep, no alcohol/cannabis, gentle movement, meaningful social connection
- analogous to critical periods in developmental neuroscience

**key papers:**
- Carhart-Harris, R.L. & Friston, K.J. (2019). ["REBUS and the Anarchic Brain."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31104773/) *Pharmacological Reviews*, 71(3), 316-344.
- Ly, C., et al. (2018). ["Psychedelics Promote Structural and Functional Neural Plasticity."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29881536/) *Cell Reports*, 23(11), 3170-3182.
- Barrett, F.S., et al. (2020). ["Psilocybin acutely alters the functional connectivity of the claustrum..."](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32101653/) *NeuroImage*.

### 2.2 safety profile

**contraindications (consensus across all sources):**

| contraindication | risk | severity |
|---|---|---|
| lithium | seizures | **CRITICAL — absolute contraindication** |
| personal/family history of psychosis | triggered/worsened psychosis | **high** |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | serotonin syndrome risk, blunted effects | **moderate-high — requires medical supervision** |
| MAOIs | dangerous serotonin interaction | **high** |
| tramadol | seizure risk | **high** |
| cardiovascular conditions | transient BP/HR elevation | **moderate** |
| pregnancy | insufficient safety data | **unknown — avoid** |
| cannabis co-use | amplified anxiety/paranoia | **moderate** |

**toxicological safety:** psilocybin has an extremely high LD50 — essentially no risk of lethal overdose from the mushroom itself. the risks are psychological, not physiological.

**sources:**
- [UCSF: Medical Contraindications to Classic Psychedelic Use](https://psychedelics.ucsf.edu/blog/medical-contraindications-to-classic-psychedelic-use)
- [PMC: Drug-Drug Interactions with Classic Psychedelics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10851641/)

## 4. the polarity model

### 4.1 the energetic arc of psychedelic work

this is mycelium's original contribution. no existing framework maps the psychedelic arc to feminine/masculine energy polarity.

**preparation = predominantly masculine energy**
structure, discipline, screening, commitment, daily practices, intention-setting

**the experience = predominantly feminine energy**
surrender, receive, feel, be present, trust. "the most important skill is a feminine one: being with what arises without trying to fix it."

**integration = both, in sequence**
- first weeks: feminine (be with what arose, journal without analyzing, rest, RAIN method)
- weeks 2-8: blend (identify patterns + continue feeling, IFS parts dialogue)
- ongoing: masculine (behavioral commitments, lifestyle changes, accountability)
- the spiral continues: new feminine openings reveal new material, requiring new masculine structure

maps to psychedelic integration because material is often overwhelming, preverbal, or deeply embodied. RAIN provides structured feminine practice.

### 6.2 the 8 ethical principles (Lancet 2023)

1. **reverence** — treat medicines as sacred kin within ecosystems
2. **respect** — engage indigenous scholars; implement FPIC
3. **responsibility** — prevent appropriation; equitable benefit-sharing
4. **relevance** — indigenous perspectives in institutional positions
5. **regulation** — legal protections for indigenous IP
6. **reparation** — fair compensation and indigenous control
7. **restoration** — indigenous authority over their medicines
8. **reconciliation** — restorative and transformative justice

source: [PMC: Ethical Principles of Traditional Indigenous Medicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9950658/)

### 6.3 design rules for mycelium

**do:**
- credit traditions by name: "inspired by Mazatec preparation practices"

## 7. music as therapeutic variable

### 7.1 the hidden therapist

**landmark finding (Kaelen et al., 2018):** the likelihood of depression reduction depended on a positive response to the music, but NOT on the intensity of the drug effects. music is not background — it is the primary non-pharmacological therapeutic variable.

**three factors predicting positive music response:**
1. **liking** — the person likes the music
2. **resonance** — it feels aligned with their emotional state
3. **openness** — they feel open to what the music evokes

source: Kaelen, M. et al. (2018). ["The hidden therapist: evidence for a central role of music in psychedelic therapy."](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5893695/) *Psychopharmacology*.

### 7.2 the JHU psilocybin playlist

**6-phase structure (7 hours 40 minutes):**

| phase | character | examples |
|---|---|---|
| arrival | gentle, ambient | sets room tone |
| onset | light classical, grounding | supports inward transition |
| ascent | building intensity | Barber's "Adagio for Strings" |
| peak | expansive, transcendent | Gorecki's "Symphony of Sorrowful Songs" (27 min) |
| descent | warmer, gentler | emotionally supportive |
| welcome back | grounding, familiar | re-orients to ordinary consciousness |

**critical rule:** no lyrics in a language understood by the participant during peak.

**genre comparison (Barrett et al., 2021):** overtone-based playlists (Tibetan bowls, ragas, drone) produced higher mystical experience scores than Western classical. 6/10 participants chose overtone when given a choice. **there is no single correct genre — person-centered selection matters.**

**sources:**
- [Hopkins Medicine: Inside the Psilocybin Playlist](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/news/articles/2020/10/inside-the-johns-hopkins-psilocybin-playlist)
- [JHU Psilocybin Playlist on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7aVExA8Lb72NFNbRBZfJLJ)
- Barrett, F.S. et al. (2017). ["Qualitative and Quantitative Features of Music..."](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01238/full) *Frontiers in Psychology*.
- [Wavepaths: Adaptive Music for Psychedelic Therapy](https://wavepaths.com/music-for-psychedelic-therapy)
- [MAPS: Creating a Psilocybin Session Playlist](https://maps.org/news/bulletin/creating-psilocybin-session-playlist/)

### 7.3 music as preparation and integration

**preparation:**
- pre-session playlist familiarization (2 weeks before)
- intention-setting while listening to specific tracks
- music autobiography (share musical history with guide/tool)
- breathwork with music (holotropic 3-phase structure)

**integration:**
- revisiting session playlist the next day (re-activates memories)
- track-by-track journaling
- building a personal "integration playlist" linked to journal entries
- somatic processing with session music (gentle movement)

**for open-source tool:** link to playlists on Spotify/Apple Music (no licensing issues). use CC-licensed original compositions for embedded audio (breathwork, meditation).

## 8. legal, ethics, and harm reduction

### 8.1 legal positioning

**mycelium must be an education/information tool, NOT medical/therapeutic.**

required disclaimers (modeled on [Fireside Project terms](https://firesideproject.org/terms-of-use)):
- "this tool does not encourage or condone any illegal activities"
- "does NOT provide medical care, medical assessments, psychotherapy, or any professional service"
- "not a substitute for medical, psychiatric, or emergency services"
- "users bear all risks and are solely responsible for informing themselves"
- geo-aware: "psychedelic substances have different legal statuses in different jurisdictions"

**screening language must be educational, never diagnostic:**
- ✅ "people with these conditions should consult their healthcare provider"
- ❌ "based on your answers, you should not proceed"

### 8.3 ethical framework

**zendo project's 4 principles (mycelium's philosophical foundation):**
1. **create a safe space** — calm, comfortable, minimal stimulation
2. **sitting, not guiding** — respect autonomy. the tool does not direct.
3. **talk through, not down** — help process, don't suppress
4. **difficult is not the same as bad** — challenging = growth opportunity

source: [Zendo Project](https://zendoproject.org/)

**MAPS code of ethics — relevant sections:**
- safety: protecting user welfare as primary obligation
- privacy: psychedelic use data could result in employment, housing, custody discrimination
- transparency: full disclosure of methods, risks, limitations

source: [MAPS Code of Ethics](https://maps.org/news/bulletin/maps-mdma-assisted-psychotherapy-code-of-ethics-spring-2019/)

### 8.5 privacy architecture

psychedelic use data is legally sensitive. design decisions:

- NEVER store identifiable information linked to substance use
- data architecture must be defensible if subpoenaed
